Privacy policy

Habesha Names · Last updated: 27 November 2026

What we collect

  • Anonymous account. The app signs you in anonymously (Firebase Authentication) so your favorites and partner link can sync. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Favorites and notes you save, swipe likes shared with a partner you link with, and family votes you create (the names on the ballot and the names family members type when they vote).
  • Things you send us: name suggestions and AI-waitlist requests (the text you type, the app language and the features you chose).
  • Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase): events such as searches, favorites, swipes, matches, shares and purchases, plus device and app information.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): technical details about errors so we can fix them.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob) in the free version, which may use device identifiers to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

How we use it

To run the app’s features (sync, partner matches, family votes, notifications), to improve the name catalogue, to fix crashes, and to understand which features are useful. We don’t sell your data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we send a daily “name of the day” and, if you joined the waitlist, an announcement when AI features launch. You can turn them off in Settings or in your phone’s settings.

Sharing

Data is stored with Google Firebase. Your partner sees the names you like while you are linked. People who open a family-vote link see the names on that ballot. Nothing else is shared with other users.

Your choices

You can unlink your partner, remove favorites, and close a family vote at any time. To delete all data linked to your anonymous account, follow the steps on our data deletion page.

Children

The app is meant for parents and families choosing a name. It is not directed at children.
