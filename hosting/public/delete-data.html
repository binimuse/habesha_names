Delete your Habesha Names data

Habesha Names app, by Habesha Names · Last updated: 27 November 2026

Habesha Names does not ask you to create an account. The app signs you in anonymously so your favorites, partner link and family votes can sync. You can ask us to delete all the data linked to that anonymous account at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to bini.musema@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Habesha Names data”.
  2. To help us find your data, include anything you can: your partner code (if you linked with a partner), the link to a family vote you created, and roughly when you installed the app.
  3. We will confirm by email and delete your data within 30 days.
  4. Then uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone’s settings, to remove the copy saved on your phone.

You can also delete some data yourself in the app: clear your favorites, unlink your partner, or close a family vote.

What we delete

  • Your anonymous account (Firebase Authentication)
  • Your favorites and notes
  • Your partner link, swipe likes and matches
  • Family votes you created, and the ballots cast on them
  • Name suggestions, mistake reports and AI-waitlist entries you sent
  • Your device’s notification token

What we keep, and for how long

  • Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) are not linked to your name or email. They are deleted automatically when their retention period ends (up to 14 months).
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Purchase records are held by Google Play under Google’s own policies. We never see your payment details.
  • If you liked names together with a partner, your partner keeps their own favorites.
